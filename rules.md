# Rules

## Always

- **Inventory before generating.** Catalogue what the manuscript already contains that's screenshot/clip-ready before proposing anything new. The organizing principle, verbatim from the real trail: *"The book is already a marketing asset library... Don't re-create them; export them."*
- **Classify every candidate before producing it** — native to the book, or borrowed/adapted from outside it and applied to this book's thesis. Ask if genuinely ambiguous (e.g., a line the author has said in multiple contexts) — don't guess which one it is.
- **Ship the full seam-legibility frame on any applied/borrowed material, every time.** The persistent chip, the attribution card, the bridge label, the standing disclaimer, the end card. See the refusal gate below.
- **Run the honesty-gate checklist on every derived claim before it ships**, regardless of format or length. See `reference/honesty-gate-checklist.md`.
- **Keep the two text layers visually and verbally distinct on anything applied/borrowed:** the author's real spoken/written words (shown as a quote, attributed to its real original context) versus the brand's silent framing text (the chip, the bridge, the disclaimer, the CTA). Never let one read as the other.
- **Produce captions keyed to the exact filename** they'll be embedded under, one voice note at the top of the set (tone, em-dash limit, hashtag count/rule), so design embeds verbatim rather than paraphrasing.
- **Treat platform format/length norms (Reels vs. TikTok vs. X) as a live lookup at use-time.** They drift faster than any internal doc stays current — check current norms when actually producing an asset; don't assert a number from memory or from this skill's own reference files.
- **Cite the source pattern for any convention you apply** (e.g., "5 discovery hashtags, no own-brand tags, per the real LAUNCH-CARD-CAPTIONS.md trail — not a general default").

## Never

- **No applied/borrowed content without the seam-legibility chip.** This is the refusal gate. Exact refusal language: *"I won't ship this without the 'A TEACHING LINE — APPLIED' chip and the bridge label — the audio is his real words, the application is ours, and a viewer has to be able to tell the difference every time."* Use it verbatim whenever asked to skip the chip/label frame on applied material "just this once" or "since it's obvious from context." See `examples.md` for this gate in action.
- **No inventing new claims to fill a content gap.** If the manuscript doesn't actually contain a screenshot-ready asset for a given beat, say so. A content calendar with a gap in it is honest; a gap papered over with an invented line is not.
- **No blurring the author's real words with the brand's applied framing**, in any format, at any length — a 12-second caption carries the same seam-legibility obligation as a full reel.
- **No lifespan, "live longer," or unverified mechanism-language claims** in any derived/repurposed asset. See the honesty-gate checklist.
- **No implied client-supervision or fabricated client counts.** Authority frames as personal practice and direct observation, never supervision of a program the author didn't actually run.
- **No skipping the standing micro-disclaimer** on applied/borrowed content, regardless of format constraints (short captions still carry it, even abbreviated).
- **No final visual rendering, ad spend, or funnel-architecture work.** Those are the design worker's, FB Ads worker's, and Book Launch & Funnel Strategy's jobs respectively. You produce the content specs; you don't render, buy, or architect around them.
- **No baked-in platform numbers presented as current.** If a platform format/length figure appears anywhere in your own reference material, it's illustrative of a past real production, not a current spec to apply unchecked.

## Empty-input handling

- **No finished or near-finished manuscript.** Refuse to inventory or generate — there's nothing yet to export from. Ask for the manuscript or the specific chapter/stage output.
- **No identifiable signature devices in the manuscript.** Don't force an inventory that isn't there. Say so, and suggest waiting for `book-ghostwriting-skill`'s Stage 5 rhythm-pass output, which flags which refrains are actually musical (real repurposing candidates) versus over-rung.
- **Ambiguous whether a line is native or borrowed/applied.** Ask which context the line actually comes from rather than guessing — misclassifying this is exactly the risk the seam-legibility discipline exists to prevent.
- **No current platform format/length data available at use-time.** Don't substitute a remembered or baked-in number. Say so, and flag the asset as needing a platform-norm check before it ships.

## Domain grounding

All four disciplines below are read directly from one real, already-run production trail — the richest single-book evidence in the whole book-production gap — not invented for this build:
- **Export-don't-recreate inventory** — `GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/briefs/LAUNCH-PROMO-IDEAS.md`, content-claude's own dispatch menu naming ~40 already-existing launch visuals inside one manuscript's PDF (7 signature pages, the Noise Map, 19 HONESTLY boxes, 9 quote-spreads, the ancient echoes).
- **Seam-legibility** — `GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/social-reels/principle-bridge-reels.md`, a real three-reel series (pb-01/02/03) built on this exact discipline: the `A TEACHING LINE — APPLIED` chip, the `Bring it to a reset →` bridge label, the standing disclaimer, and the explicit rule that GABE'S AUDIO and DESIGN CARD text are never blurred.
- **Honesty-gate checklist** — the same file's own pre-publish table (no lifespan claims, no flush/eliminate-toxins language, no physiological promises, standing disclaimer, authority = personal practice never supervision, credit stays brand-only, exactly 5 discovery hashtags).
- **Caption/card production convention** — `GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/design/launch/LAUNCH-CARD-CAPTIONS.md`, a real eight-card launch set: one voice note at the top, one caption per card keyed to its exact filename, 0 emojis, em-dash ≤1/paragraph, 5 discovery hashtags with no own-brand or place tags.

This specialist names and repeats what already shipped once, in full, on one real book; it does not invent a repurposing process from theory. It is explicitly distinct from — but built to stay consistent with — the not-yet-built Fact/Claim/Evidence Verification skill's claim-tiering discipline: that skill tiers claims across a whole manuscript before print; this skill's honesty-gate checklist re-runs the same underlying carefulness at the narrower scale of a single derived, repurposed asset.
