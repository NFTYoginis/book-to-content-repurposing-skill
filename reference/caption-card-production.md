# Caption/card production

The output format once an asset has been inventoried, classified, and (if applied/borrowed) framed for seam-legibility. Read directly from `GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/design/launch/LAUNCH-CARD-CAPTIONS.md`, a real eight-card launch-caption set design embedded verbatim.

## The convention

**One voice note at the top of the set, not repeated per card.** States, once, for the whole batch:
- **Tone** — the register every caption in the set should hold (e.g. "unsentimental, physiology over mysticism, care-first, no hype").
- **Em-dash limit** — a hard cap per paragraph (the real trail: ≤1/paragraph).
- **Emoji rule** — the real trail's own: 0 emojis.
- **Hashtag count/rule** — an exact number and a scope rule (the real trail: exactly 5 discovery hashtags per caption, no own-brand or place tags).
- **CTA convention** — where every caption in the set points (the real trail: one consistent URL, stated once as the standing rule rather than re-derived per card).

**Then one caption per card, keyed to its exact filename.** Format: `## N — \`path/to/exact-filename.png\`` followed by a one-line parenthetical description of what the asset actually shows, then the caption body, then a `**First comment:**` line with the hashtags. Design embeds this **verbatim** — the caption is not a starting point for design to paraphrase, it's the finished text.

## Why filename-keying matters

A caption that isn't tied to its exact asset filename creates a matching problem the moment there's more than one card in a set — design either has to guess which caption goes with which image, or ask, and either path reintroduces the paraphrase risk this convention exists to avoid. Key every caption to its filename even for a single-asset job; it costs nothing when there's one card, and it's load-bearing the moment there's more than one.

## Caption shape (from the real eight-card set)

Each real caption follows roughly the same internal shape, though not rigidly: open with the hook or the quoted line itself, develop the idea in 1–2 short paragraphs (the "honest reframe" — what the common assumption gets wrong, what the book's own material actually says), close with a short CTA line and the standing URL. Length runs 3–4 short paragraphs for a feed caption; shorter formats (see below) compress this, they don't restructure it.

## Platform format — a live lookup, not a baked-in number

Reels, TikTok, and X format/length norms (character limits, ideal caption length, hashtag placement conventions) drift faster than this file stays current. When actually producing an asset for a specific platform, check the current norm at use-time rather than assuming the real trail's own numbers (which were correct for Instagram/TikTok as of the real production date) still hold. See `rules.md`'s Always section.

## Worked reference — the real voice note and one full card

**Real voice note, verbatim in shape:** *"Voice: [book's own thematic frame]; unsentimental, physiology over mysticism, "with, not to." Honesty is the trust signal. 0 emojis · em-dash ≤1/paragraph · care-first, no hype... Each 'First comment:' line = 5 discovery hashtags, no own-brand or place tags."*

**One real card, in full shape:**

> ## 8 — `series/myth-honest/myth-01.png`
> *(MYTH: Detox flushes toxins out of your body. HONESTLY: your liver and kidneys already do that — a fast changes your awareness, not your blood chemistry.)*
>
> Myth: detox flushes the toxins out of your body.
>
> Honestly? Your liver and kidneys already do that, every minute, whether you fast or not. No juice, tea, or cleanse protocol meaningfully outperforms organs that have been filtering your blood your entire life.
>
> So what does a fast actually change? Your awareness, not your blood chemistry... A debunk is worth as much as a promise. [URL]
>
> **First comment:** [5 discovery hashtags, no own-brand or place tags]

Note that this card doubles as an honesty-gate pass in miniature — it corrects a mechanism claim rather than asserting one, and its closing line ("a debunk is worth as much as a promise") states the book's own honesty positioning directly. Run `reference/honesty-gate-checklist.md` against every card regardless — this one happens to pass cleanly by construction, but that's not something to assume for every card.
