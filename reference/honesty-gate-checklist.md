# Honesty-gate checklist

Run before any repurposed or derived-claim asset ships — regardless of format or length. Read directly from `GabeYoga-HQ/Detox Book/social-reels/principle-bridge-reels.md`'s own pre-publish checklist table, built for research-claude's honesty gate and reused here as this skill's own discipline.

## Why this exists as its own step

Repurposing compresses. A claim that's carefully scoped across a full chapter can lose its qualifiers when it's cut down to a 12-second caption — the same discipline that kept the manuscript honest has to survive that compression, deliberately, not by accident. This checklist is the repurposing-specific analogue of the manuscript-extraction stage's claim-sensitivity flag: same underlying carefulness, applied at the scale of one derived asset instead of one whole book.

**Distinct from Fact/Claim/Evidence Verification (not yet built):** that skill tiers every factual claim in a manuscript — strong, moderate, speculative — before print, at the scale of the whole book. This checklist runs at the scale of a single repurposed asset, after that tiering has already happened upstream (or, if it hasn't, using the same underlying honesty standard informally). The two are built to stay consistent, not to duplicate each other: if Fact/Claim/Evidence Verification has already tiered a claim as speculative, a repurposed asset built from it inherits that tier and can't quietly present it as strong.

## The checklist

| Constraint | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Principle-bridge framing, not corpus claim** (for applied/borrowed material) | Every asset built on applied material carries the full seam-legibility frame — see `reference/seam-legibility.md`. The application is a DESIGN CARD, never presented as something the author said. |
| **No lifespan / "live longer" claims** | Absent from the copy entirely — not softened, not hedged, removed. |
| **No "removes / flushes / eliminates" mechanism language** unless the manuscript itself makes and supports that exact claim | If the underlying mechanism claim is actually a correction (a myth being debunked), the copy should read as the correction, not restate the myth as fact. |
| **No unverified physiological promises** (heal, cure, "resets your gut," etc.) | A perception/awareness claim ("you start noticing your patterns") is not the same as a physiological claim ("this changes your gut biome") — keep the two distinct, and don't let a compressed caption blur one into the other. |
| **Standing micro-disclaimer present** | On-screen/on-page footer plus in the caption text itself, every asset, every time — not just the ones that feel like they need it. |
| **Authority frame — personal practice / direct observation, never supervision** | No language implying the author ran, supervised, or administered a program for anyone. "He noticed," "he observed," "his own practice" — never "he treated," "his clients," "under his supervision." |
| **No fabricated client counts or outcome numbers** | If a number isn't sourced from the manuscript or the author directly, it doesn't appear. |
| **Credit stays brand-only on public-facing copy** | No personal name surfaced on the asset itself unless the manuscript or brief explicitly calls for it. |
| **Hashtag/format convention followed** | See `reference/caption-card-production.md` for the exact count and no-own-brand rule; a platform's current format specifics are a live lookup, not something to assert from memory. |

## How to run it

Go line by line against the actual finished asset text — not against the intent behind it. A caption that means to stay in the "awareness, not physiology" lane but slips into "resets your gut" fails this checklist even if the writer's intent was correct; the checklist checks what the words actually say, not what they were meant to say.

## Worked reference — a real check, item by item

Against pb-03 (*"All I'm really doing is helping you build awareness around yourself..."*): principle-bridge framing present (chip, attribution naming it as his teaching ethos, bridge explicitly reframing to "a reset"); no lifespan claim; no flush/eliminate language; no physiological promise (the copy's own claim is "awareness," not a biological outcome); standing disclaimer present in both on-screen footer and caption text; authority framed as "no one supervising you... not a program you're supervised through" — an explicit anti-supervision statement, not just an absence of one; no client numbers; credit stays brand-only; hashtags at exactly 5, all discovery tags, none own-brand. Every line of the checklist has a specific, checkable answer in the finished asset — not a general assurance that it's "probably fine."
